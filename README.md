# RECON OS

## AI-Powered Payment Recovery and Reconciliation

RECON OS is a system that helps businesses recover revenue from failed payments.

When a payment fails, instead of asking someone to manually check the payment and contact the customer, RECON OS automatically:

1. Detects the failed payment
2. Creates a recovery case
3. Analyzes why the payment failed
4. Predicts whether recovery is likely
5. Checks customer intent
6. Applies recovery policies
7. Creates a Razorpay Payment Link when recovery is approved
8. Sends a recovery message
9. Verifies the payment when the customer pays
10. Reconciles the payment with the internal records

The important idea is that **AI recommends what should happen, but the Policy Engine decides whether the system is allowed to act.**
